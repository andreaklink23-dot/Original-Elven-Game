Long ago, the Sunken Crown — relic of the first Elven Queen — was shattered into five Crown Shards and scattered across Aethermoor by the warlord Varek the Unmade. Each shard corrupts the land where it fell, twisting beasts and breaking the seasons.

You are the last of the Brightblood lineage — heir to a gift that lets you hear the shards calling. Journey through four cursed territories, reclaim the Shards, and reforge the Crown before Varek's shadow devours the last of the light.
